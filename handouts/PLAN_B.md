# Plan B: if the internet fails

## Before the day
- Save **From_Ideas_to_Impact_OFFLINE.html** (in this folder) on your laptop **and** on a USB stick.
- Print the handouts: one proposal template per team (+ spares), one cheat sheet per team, your cue card, and `QUESTIONS.md` (answer key).
- Turn on your phone's mobile hotspot once to make sure it works with your laptop.
- Optional: print 60 small slips with the house names (12 houses × 5) in case registration cannot run.

## On the day, open the live site early
Open `https://ideas-to-impact.learneasytoday.workers.dev/?host` on the laptop before people arrive. Once it is open, **do not reload or close the tab**: the slides, calculators, and timers keep working even if the internet drops.

## If something fails

| What fails | What still works | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Venue Wi-Fi only | Phones on mobile data keep answering | Connect the laptop to your phone's hotspot. Nothing is lost. |
| A participant's phone drops | Their typed answer stays on the phone | It sends by itself when the phone reconnects ("Reconnecting…" bar). |
| Laptop internet, tab still open | Slides, calculators, timers | Pushing questions and live results pause. Ask by show of hands until the hotspot is on. |
| Laptop internet, tab closed or reloaded | — | Open **From_Ideas_to_Impact_OFFLINE.html** (double-click), tap **Present offline (slides only)**. |
| Everything (no power to the router, no data) | The offline file and the printed handouts | Run the session from the offline file. Phone questions become show of hands; habit checks are already fingers up. |

## Without phones
- **Registration and houses:** count people per barangay by show of hands; hand out house slips (Mix: deal them across barangays; Same barangay: give each barangay its own houses).
- **Phone questions:** read the question from the slide or `QUESTIONS.md`; teams answer by raising hands or holding up fingers.
- **Team work:** the team work slides and their timers work offline; teams write on the printed proposal template.
