# Question bank: facilitator answer key

51 questions are built into the presenter screen. On any slide, open **Question bank and ask now**, pick the segment, and tap **Push**. Answers are not shown on the projector; they appear on screen and on phones only when you tap **Show result on phones**. Habit checks follow the cues in the talk outline.

Keep this page for yourself.

## Opening

- *Text* — In one word: how do you feel when you plan an SK activity?
- *Choice* — Which part of an activity is hardest for your SK? [Planning the activity / Making the budget / Thinking about the risks] (opinion poll)
- *Rating 1–5* — How confident are you in writing an activity proposal? 1 = not yet, 5 = very confident
- *Choice* — In the 7 Habits, which habit is in the middle? [Be Proactive / Think Win-Win / Synergize / Sharpen the Saw] **Answer: Think Win-Win**
- *Text* — Name one SK activity idea you have. What would make it fail?
- *Choice* — Habit check: "Listen first" is which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 5 Seek First to Understand**

## Part 1 · Plan Responsibly

- *Choice* — "Hold a basketball league" is… [An activity, not yet an objective / A strong objective / A KPI / A risk] **Answer: An activity, not yet an objective**
- *Choice* — In the objective formula, what comes right after "so that"? [The activity / The result / The budget / The date] **Answer: The result**
- *Choice* — Which part makes an objective measurable? [The KPI and target / A catchy title / The venue / The number of SK officials] **Answer: The KPI and target**
- *Text* — Write a KPI you could use for a barangay clean-up drive.
- *Choice* — When do you fill in the Actual column of the evaluation? [Before the activity / During the planning meeting / After the activity / Only if something goes wrong] **Answer: After the activity**
- *Choice* — Habit check: You wrote the result before you planned a single activity. Which habit is that? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 2 Begin with the End in Mind**
- *Choice* — Habit check: Before deciding what the youth need, you asked the youth. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 5 Seek First to Understand**
- *Choice* — Your objective is attendance. Which is the right evidence? [A sign-in sheet / The number of photos taken / A thank-you post online / The budget total] **Answer: A sign-in sheet**
- *Choice* — Some participants are under 18. What does that trigger? [Guardian consent / A bigger budget / A new venue / Nothing] **Answer: Guardian consent**

## Part 2 · Budget Transparently

- *Number* — Meals: 40 participants × ₱200. What is the total for meals? **Answer: 8,000**
- *Number* — Total budget ₱18,000 for 40 participants. What is the cost per participant? **Answer: 450**
- *Number* — Budget ₱12,000. Cost per participant ₱400. How many participants can we serve? **Answer: 30**
- *Choice* — The total budget is… [A result of the itemized list / The starting point / Whatever is left in the fund / Decided by the venue] **Answer: A result of the itemized list**
- *Choice* — The budget is cut. What do you cut LAST? [Safety items like first aid and headcounts / Snacks and extras / Giveaways / Decorations] **Answer: Safety items like first aid and headcounts**
- *Choice* — Your risk action costs money. Where does that cost go? [Its own line in the budget / Nowhere, it is free / Taken from the meals / Paid by participants] **Answer: Its own line in the budget**
- *Choice* — Habit check: You decided what gets funded first and what can wait. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 3 Put First Things First**
- *Choice* — "Food, PHP 5,000" is… [A lump sum, not a budget line / A proper budget line] **Answer: A lump sum, not a budget line**
- *Choice* — Who checks the budget sheet before it is submitted? [The SK Treasurer / The venue owner / The participants / No one] **Answer: The SK Treasurer**

## Part 3 · Manage Risks Proactively

- *Number* — Occurrence 3 × Impact 4. What is the Risk Rating? **Answer: 12**
- *Choice* — A Risk Rating of 12 is in which band? [Low / Medium / High / Extreme] **Answer: High**
- *Choice* — A Risk Rating of 2 (Low). What is the treatment? [Accept / Reduce / Transfer / Share / Avoid] **Answer: Accept**
- *Choice* — A Risk Rating of 20 (Extreme). What is the treatment? [Accept / Reduce / Avoid; the Committee decides / Ignore it] **Answer: Avoid; the Committee decides**
- *Choice* — Additional Controls lower which number? [Occurrence / Impact / The budget / The number of participants] **Answer: Occurrence**
- *Choice* — Mitigation lowers which number? [Occurrence / Impact / The budget / The number of participants] **Answer: Impact**
- *Choice* — A participant needs a clinic visit but is not hospitalized. What is the Impact? [1 Negligible / 2 Minor / 3 Moderate / 4 Significant] **Answer: 2 Minor**
- *Choice* — The activity gets regional media attention and major complaints. What is the Impact? [2 Minor / 3 Moderate / 4 Significant / 5 Severe] **Answer: 3 Moderate**
- *Choice* — When can you Accept a risk? [Only when the Risk Rating is already Low / When it is Medium / When the budget is tight / Whenever the team agrees] **Answer: Only when the Risk Rating is already Low**
- *Text* — Name one Additional Control for rain on the day of an outdoor activity.
- *Risk* — Describe one risk for your activity, then rate Occurrence and Impact as it stands today.
- *Choice* — Habit check: You are fixing the problem before it happens instead of reacting. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 1 Be Proactive**
- *Choice* — Habit check: You want the Committee, the venue owner, and the youth all to say yes. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 4 Think Win-Win**
- *Choice* — "First-aid kit on site" is… [A control / A mitigation / An action] **Answer: A control**
- *Choice* — "Safety officer buys and checks the first-aid kit by Nov 20" is… [A control / A mitigation / An action] **Answer: An action**
- *Choice* — Who should be the owner of a risk? [One named person / Everyone / The whole SK / Whoever is free on the day] **Answer: One named person**
- *Choice* — After controls, the Residual Risk Rating is still High. What now? [The Committee must approve before the activity proceeds / Proceed anyway / Accept it / Delete the risk] **Answer: The Committee must approve before the activity proceeds**

## Peer review and presentations

- *Text* — What is one strong point of the plan you just reviewed?
- *Rating 1–5* — Rate the plan you just heard. 1 = needs work, 5 = ready to implement.
- *Choice* — Habit check: Another team caught a risk you missed, and your draft got better. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 6 Synergize**
- *Choice* — After the proponent signs, who pre-approves the proposal? [The Risk and Safety Committee / The Barangay Council / The SK Treasurer / The participants] **Answer: The Risk and Safety Committee**
