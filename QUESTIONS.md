# Question bank: facilitator answer key

53 questions are built into the presenter screen. On any slide, open **Question bank and ask now** and tap **Push**. The bank only offers questions about what you have already presented: each question unlocks at the slide named in brackets. A pushed question leaves the list (tap **Show pushed** to repeat one; **Clear all answers** resets the list). Answers are not shown on the projector; they appear on screen and on phones only when you tap **Show result on phones**. Habit checks follow the cues in the talk outline.

Risk Rating = Occurrence + Impact (2 to 10), per the office Risk Legend. 6 and below: Accept. Above 6: Reduce, Transfer / Share, or Avoid, and the Committee must approve.

Keep this page for yourself.

## Opening (12 min)

- *Text* (from "From Ideas to Impact") — In one word: how do you feel when you plan an SK activity?
- *Choice* (from "What was missing?") — Which part of an activity is hardest for your SK? [Planning the activity / Making the budget / Thinking about the risks] (opinion poll)
- *Rating 1–5* (from "From Ideas to Impact") — How confident are you in writing an activity proposal? 1 = not yet, 5 = very confident
- *Choice* (from "The 7 Habits, as a memory device") — In the 7 Habits, which habit is in the middle? [Be Proactive / Think Win-Win / Synergize / Sharpen the Saw] **Answer: Think Win-Win**
- *Text* (from "The event that went wrong") — Name one SK activity idea you have. What would make it fail?
- *Choice* (from "The 7 Habits, as a memory device") — Habit check: "Listen first" is which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 5 Seek First to Understand**

## Part 1 · Plan Responsibly (24 min)

- *Choice, on the "Start with the result, not the activity" slide* — Start with the result, not the activity. Which habit is that? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 2 Begin with the End in Mind**
- *Choice* (from "Start with the result, not the activity") — "Hold a basketball league" is… [An activity, not yet an objective / A strong objective / A KPI / A risk] **Answer: An activity, not yet an objective**
- *Choice* (from "Build an objective") — In the objective formula, what comes right after "so that"? [The activity / The result / The budget / The date] **Answer: The result**
- *Choice* (from "Build an objective") — Which part makes an objective measurable? [The KPI and target / A catchy title / The venue / The number of SK officials] **Answer: The KPI and target**
- *Text* (from "Build an objective") — Write a KPI you could use for a barangay clean-up drive.
- *Choice* (from "Objective, then evaluation") — When do you fill in the Actual column of the evaluation? [Before the activity / During the planning meeting / After the activity / Only if something goes wrong] **Answer: After the activity**
- *Choice* (from "Start with the result, not the activity") — Habit check: You wrote the result before you planned a single activity. Which habit is that? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 2 Begin with the End in Mind**
- *Choice* (from "Start with the result, not the activity") — Habit check: Before deciding what the youth need, you asked the youth. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 5 Seek First to Understand**
- *Choice* (from "Objective, then evaluation") — Your objective is attendance. Which is the right evidence? [A sign-in sheet / The number of photos taken / A thank-you post online / The budget total] **Answer: A sign-in sheet**
- *Choice* (from "Team work: page 1") — Some participants are under 18. What does that trigger? [Guardian consent / A bigger budget / A new venue / Nothing] **Answer: Guardian consent**

## Part 2 · Budget Transparently (16 min)

- *Choice* (from "Itemize everything") — The total budget is… [A result of the itemized list / The starting point / Whatever is left in the fund / Decided by the venue] **Answer: A result of the itemized list**
- *Choice* (from ""What if the budget is just this much?"") — The budget is cut. What must stay in the budget no matter what? [Safety items like first aid and headcounts / Snacks and extras / Giveaways / Decorations] **Answer: Safety items like first aid and headcounts**
- *Choice* (from "Itemize everything") — Your risk action costs money. Where does that cost go? [Its own line in the budget / Nowhere, it is free / Taken from the meals / Paid by participants] **Answer: Its own line in the budget**
- *Choice* (from ""What if the budget is just this much?"") — Habit check: You decided what gets funded first and what can wait. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 3 Put First Things First**
- *Choice* (from "Make it checkable") — "Food, PHP 5,000" is… [A lump sum, not a budget line / A proper budget line] **Answer: A lump sum, not a budget line**
- *Choice* (from "Make it checkable") — What goes in the Remarks column? [The assumption behind the number, like "30 participants plus 5 staff" / Your name / The date of the activity / Nothing, leave it blank] **Answer: The assumption behind the number, like "30 participants plus 5 staff"**
- *Choice* (from "Make it checkable") — Where should the approved budget go? [Where the community can see it / In the Treasurer's drawer only / Nowhere, it is private / Only on the Chairperson's phone] **Answer: Where the community can see it**
- *Choice* (from "Make it checkable") — What do you attach for larger items? [Quotations / Nothing / A photo of the venue / The participant list] **Answer: Quotations**
- *Text* (from "Itemize, total, cost per participant") — What does PHP 450 per participant buy in your plan, and is that where the money should go?
- *Choice* (from "Make it checkable") — Who checks the budget sheet before it is submitted? [The SK Treasurer / The venue owner / The participants / No one] **Answer: The SK Treasurer**

## Part 3 · Manage Risks Proactively (40 min)

- *Number* (from "How big is the risk?") — Occurrence 3 + Impact 4. What is the Risk Rating? **Answer: 7**
- *Choice* (from "What do we do with the rating?") — A Risk Rating of 5. What is the treatment? [Accept / Reduce, Transfer / Share, or Avoid] **Answer: Accept**
- *Choice* (from "What do we do with the rating?") — A Risk Rating of 8. What is the treatment? [Accept / Reduce, Transfer / Share, or Avoid] **Answer: Reduce, Transfer / Share, or Avoid**
- *Choice* (from "What do we do with the rating?") — A Risk Rating of 7. Does the Committee need to approve? [Yes, it is above 6 / No] **Answer: Yes, it is above 6**
- *Choice* (from "What do we do with the rating?") — A Risk Rating of 6. What is the treatment? [Accept / Reduce, Transfer / Share, or Avoid] **Answer: Accept**
- *Choice* (from "Four words first") — Additional Controls lower which number? [Occurrence / Impact / The budget / The number of participants] **Answer: Occurrence**
- *Choice* (from "Four words first") — Mitigation lowers which number? [Occurrence / Impact / The budget / The number of participants] **Answer: Impact**
- *Choice* (from "Impact: how bad?") — A participant needs a clinic visit but is not hospitalized. What is the Impact? [1 Negligible / 2 Minor / 3 Moderate / 4 Significant] **Answer: 2 Minor**
- *Choice* (from "Impact: how bad?") — The activity gets regional media attention and major complaints. What is the Impact? [2 Minor / 3 Moderate / 4 Significant / 5 Severe] **Answer: 3 Moderate**
- *Choice* (from "What do we do with the rating?") — When can you Accept a risk? [When the Risk Rating is 6 or below / When the Risk Rating is 12 / When the budget is tight / Whenever the team agrees] **Answer: When the Risk Rating is 6 or below**
- *Text* (from "Worked example: rain") — Name one Additional Control for rain on the day of an outdoor activity.
- *Risk* (from "Risk rating calculator") — Describe one risk for your activity, then rate Occurrence and Impact as it stands today.
- *Choice* (from "Four words first") — Habit check: You are fixing the problem before it happens instead of reacting. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 1 Be Proactive**
- *Choice* (from "What do we do with the rating?") — Habit check: You want the Committee, the venue owner, and the youth all to say yes. Which habit? [the 7 habits] **Answer: 4 Think Win-Win**
- *Choice* (from "Know the words") — "First-aid kit on site" is… [A control / A mitigation / An action] **Answer: A control**
- *Choice* (from "Know the words") — "Safety officer buys and checks the first-aid kit by Nov 20" is… [A control / A mitigation / An action] **Answer: An action**
- *Choice* (from "Know the words") — Who should be the owner of a risk? [One named person / Everyone / The whole SK / Whoever is free on the day] **Answer: One named person**
- *Choice* (from "What do we do with the rating?") — After controls, the Residual Risk Rating is still above 6. What now? [The Committee must approve before the activity proceeds / Proceed anyway / Accept it / Delete the risk] **Answer: The Committee must approve before the activity proceeds**
